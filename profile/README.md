Proof of Escrow is an open protocol for distributing tokens on the XRP Ledger to people who lock XRP in their own wallets. The XRP never leaves the owner's account, the ledger itself keeps it locked, and the longer the lock, the bigger the share. Every payout can be rebuilt from public ledger data.

It grew out of Ledger on Ledger, which ran for a year on XRP Ledger mainnet. The spec, a reference calculator and a map of how to decentralize the payouts are all open.
